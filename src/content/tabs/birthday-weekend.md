We’re creating an unforgettable weekend in Jamaica with experiences that feel
just like Janet: **beautiful, fun, stylish, and full of love.**

## Thursday, June 17 — Welcome to Jamaica

### Travel & arrival day

Check into the resort, unpack, explore, grab a cocktail, and get into island
mode.

There is no formal event planned for Thursday, giving everyone the flexibility
to arrive throughout the day and enjoy the resort at their leisure.

## Friday, June 18 — Island nights & poolside blues

### Shades of Blue Pool Party

Dive into the weekend wearing your favorite shade of blue—from sky to navy,
aqua to cobalt. Expect clear skies, cold drinks, and coordinated poolside
energy.

### White Lights

Friday evening becomes an all-white affair: a warm island welcome with
signature cocktails, island bites, live music, beachfront views, and good
people.

Final timing and resort locations will be announced.

## Saturday, June 19 — Janet’s Island in Bloom

### Animal Prints Pool Party

Unleash your wild side in bold animal prints for another unforgettable
afternoon by the pool.

### Janet’s 60th Birthday Celebration

Saturday evening is Janet’s signature celebration. Come dressed in tropical
cocktail chic for an evening of toasts, dancing, beautiful people, and
unforgettable memories.

Final timing and resort locations will be announced.

## Sunday, June 20 — Paradise, your way

Today is completely yours.

Sleep late. Order another mimosa. Head to the beach. Book a spa appointment.
Play golf. Take an excursion. Spend the afternoon at the pool.

Whatever you choose, **enjoy Jamaica!**

## Monday, June 21 — Until next time

### Official departure day

Not ready to leave? Neither are we!

Guests may work directly with our travel agent to **arrive before the official
celebration or extend their stay after June 21**, subject to resort availability
and applicable group rates.
