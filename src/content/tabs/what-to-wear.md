It’s Janet’s 60th in Jamaica, so naturally we’re bringing the looks!

This page is your guide to the attire, colors, and overall vibe for each
celebration.

## Thursday, June 17 — Arrival day

**Vibe:** Resort chic

Travel comfortably, then slip into something effortless and island-ready for
your first evening in paradise.

## Friday, June 18 — Shades of Blue

**Event:** Pool party  
**Dress code:** Shades of blue  
**Color palette:** Sky, aqua, cobalt, royal blue, and navy

Choose swimwear, cover-ups, linen, or coordinated resort pieces in your favorite
blue. Keep it cool, polished, and ready for the pool.

## Friday, June 18 — White Lights

**Event:** Island night celebration  
**Dress code:** All white  
**Vibe:** Island elegance, gold details, and warm white lights

Women may choose flowing dresses, elevated separates, or chic resort looks.
Men may choose linen, knit polos, or relaxed tailored pieces. White is the
statement; gold accessories are welcome.

## Saturday, June 19 — Animal Prints

**Event:** Pool party  
**Dress code:** Animal prints

Bring leopard, zebra, tiger, snakeskin, or another confident print to the pool.
The goal is bold, stylish, and undeniably fabulous.

## Saturday, June 19 — Island in Bloom

**Event:** Janet’s 60th birthday celebration  
**Dress code:** Tropical cocktail chic

Think vibrant florals, flowing dresses, linen sets, bold prints, colorful heels
or sandals, polished shirts, and gold accessories.

## Sunday, June 20 — Paradise, your way

**Vibe:** Resort wear

Swimwear, cover-ups, sundresses, linen, golf attire, or excursion wear—whatever
your perfect Jamaica day requires.
