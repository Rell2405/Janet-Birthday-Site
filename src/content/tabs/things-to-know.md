We’re headed to Jamaica! Before you pack your bags and grab your passport, here
are a few important things to help you prepare for a smooth, stress-free
getaway.

## 🛂 Passport & travel documents

A valid passport is required for U.S. citizens traveling to Jamaica.

Before traveling:

- Check your passport expiration date well in advance.
- Make sure the name on your airline reservation matches your passport.
- Keep a digital copy of your passport in a secure location separate from the
  original.
- Review current entry requirements before departure, as travel requirements
  can change.

Don’t wait until the last minute! If you need to apply for or renew a passport,
take care of it as early as possible.

## 📝 Jamaica entry requirements

Before arrival, travelers may be required to complete Jamaica’s official
immigration and customs documentation.

We will provide updated instructions and links closer to our departure date so
everyone has the most current information.

## 💊 Medications & health essentials

Bring enough prescription medication for your entire trip, plus a little extra
in case of unexpected travel delays.

Keep essential medications in your carry-on luggage, not your checked bag.
Whenever possible, keep prescription medications in their original labeled
containers.

Consider packing a small travel health kit with:

- Pain reliever
- Motion sickness medication
- Stomach or indigestion medication
- Allergy medication
- Sunscreen
- Insect repellent
- Bandages and basic first-aid supplies

## 📶 Cell phone & Wi-Fi

Check with your mobile carrier before traveling to determine whether your
current plan includes international service in Jamaica or if you need to add an
international travel plan.

Wi-Fi will be available at the resort, but having cellular service can be
helpful during airport transfers, excursions, and time away from the property.

## 💵 Money & spending

The Jamaican Dollar (JMD) is Jamaica’s official currency, but U.S. dollars are
widely accepted in tourist areas and resorts.

Bring small bills for tips and incidental purchases. Credit cards are also
commonly accepted, but notify your bank or credit card company of your
international travel plans if required.

## 🌦️ Island weather

June in Jamaica is warm and tropical, so plan for sunshine, heat, humidity, and
the possibility of a quick island shower.

Lightweight, breathable clothing is your friend. And yes—bring the sunscreen!
